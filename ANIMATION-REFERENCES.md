# Zelabonk animation references (previous overhaul)

For the current build’s supported moves, see [Franchise move references](FRANCHISE-MOVE-REFERENCES.md). Invented glide rigs and move examples below describe the earlier version, not current behavior.

The game uses original, procedurally drawn pixel frames and Three.js effects. Nintendo material is a design reference, not a bundled asset. Signature glides other than Link's paraglider are original moves designed for this fan game, not recreations of official abilities.

The five Ocarina of Time allies also have original full-direction sprites and glides: Impa’s veil, Saria’s leaf lift, Darunia’s ember updraft, Ruto’s water wheel, and Nabooru’s sand sash. [Nintendo’s Ocarina of Time character overview](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-2-games/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Ocarina-of-Time-3115664.html) informed the character direction. The attacks and glides are Zelabonk adaptations.

Enemy silhouettes and guardian designs reference Zelda's realms and creatures (see [Nintendo's Zelda series guide](https://zelda.nintendo.com/about/)). The timing, telegraphs and sprites are authored for this game. A melee swipe, dive, lunge and ranged spit have distinct wind-ups and follow-through. Boss patterns use their own signals for a charge, slam, volley, summon, teleport or landmark signature. Some enemies share a combat family; the animations do not imply a separate AI system for every enemy.

**Try it:** In Training Grounds, choose one of the eleven heroes, use movement and jump, then hold jump while descending to see the glide. Spawn a ranged enemy, flying enemy, elite, or guardian to see its animation states. Reduced Motion keeps the identifying glide silhouette and gameplay-critical attack cues while softening optional effects.
