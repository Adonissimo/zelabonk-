# Franchise move references

The 11 playable characters use original pixel sprites. The table identifies which official action the drawing and gameplay are based on. No Nintendo or Koei Tecmo animations, models, sound, or sprite assets are bundled. **Reference-based** does not mean identical source frames, exact original-game damage/timing, or that characters from a licensed spin-off performed the same moves in the mainline timeline. The auto-fire, cooldowns, and follower slots belong to Zelabonk.

| Hero | Official action reference |
| --- | --- |
| Link | [Ocarina of Time 3D control guide](https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/games_8/quick_start_guide/QuickStartGuide_3DS_TheLegendOfZeldaOcarinaOfTime3D_EN.pdf) for his hand-held sword and Spin Attack; [A Link Between Worlds manual](https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/manual-3DS-the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds-en) for the Master Sword beam at full hearts. The beam is Zelabonk’s animated crescent. |
| Zelda | [Nintendo’s Breath of the Wild story recap](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/relive-the-story-of-the-legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild/) for her gold sealing power; Light Arrows keep the aiming and releasing motion rather than appearing as a generic cast. |
| Midna | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/sp/characters10.html) depicts her hair-hand attacks and conjured shadow wolf. [Nintendo’s Twilight Princess HD overview](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Wii-U-games/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Twilight-Princess-HD-1082222.html) supplies the mainline character reference. Her transformation follows the [Hyrule Castle barrier scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HI0k71V57Ko), a capture from Twilight Princess. |
| Tetra | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/sp/characters25.html) documents cutlass sweeps and aimed water-pistol shots. |
| Sheik | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/sp/characters03.html) documents the knives and magical harp. This is not a claim that playing a warp song deals damage in Ocarina of Time. |
| Skull Kid | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/sp/characters20.html) documents his instruments and Tatl and Tael; [Nintendo’s Skull Kid trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5XVkLm__kT4) is a movement reference. The latest Ultimate follows the grinning falling Moon from [Majora's Mask](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-3DS-games/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Majora-s-Mask-3D-941070.html), with a targeted crash for Zelabonk. Skull Kid’s in-air pose is based on the visual reference supplied for the project. |
| Impa | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/sp/characters07.html) documents her giant blade and reverse-grip draw followed by quick strikes. |
| Saria | [Nintendo’s Ocarina of Time manual](https://m1.nintendo.net/docvc/NUS/USA/CZLE/CZLE_E.pdf) covers the original quest and equipment; her ocarina scene is a noncombat action. Any marked borrowed gear is part of Zelabonk’s starting equipment, not something the source game shows Saria using. |
| Darunia | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/sp/characters09.html) documents the giant hammer and curled Goron movement. Darunia keeps the hammer as his starter; Zelabonk’s requested Ultimate combines a charged jump, boulder pose, and damaging landing instead of a forward roll. |
| Ruto | [Koei Tecmo’s Hyrule Warriors profile](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/characters04.html) documents Zora Scale water currents and a large outgoing wave. |
| Nabooru | [Nintendo’s Ocarina of Time manual](https://m1.nintendo.net/docvc/NUS/USA/CZLE/CZLE_E.pdf) describes the sages and medallions. The Spirit Sage gesture is adapted from her medallion scene, not a solo damage spell. Her Iron Knuckle attack refers to the armored Spirit Temple encounter under Twinrova’s control, not her fighting style as a Gerudo ally. |

The eight realm guardians still have telegraphed patterns tuned for Zelabonk. Defeating a boss recruits that realm’s ally, not the boss itself. The composition, recruitment rewards, and AI assistance are fan-game systems.
