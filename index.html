Adventure

Every map has three levels. Defeat 12 enemies in level 1 and 18 in level 2. Use the central shrine to advance. Challenge the guardian in level 3. Defeat all eight, then return to Hyrule Field and challenge Ganondorf. Gates stay sealed during guardian battles.

Survival

Survive four minutes, then defeat Phantom Ganon. Realm shrines grant immediate rewards. The boss follows you through gates without recovering health.

Build your power

Collect XP. Choose weapons and charms. Rank 7 awakens a weapon. F refreshes, X skips, and Z banishes a choice. Charge your Ultimate through time and defeated enemies; unleash it with R.

Keep your progress

Safe realm checkpoints save between encounters. Boss fights restart from the previous checkpoint. Earn rupees at the end of a run, buy permanent upgrades at the Great Fairy camp, and unlock heroes by defeating guardians.
